I bought this laptop three months ago and I already regret it. The battery drains within two hours even when I am only writing documents, the fan runs constantly, and the screen flickers whenever the machine wakes from sleep. Two of the keys stopped responding in the second week, which makes typing anything longer than an email frustrating.

Support has been worse than the hardware. I opened a ticket, waited four days for a reply, and was then told to reinstall the operating system, which I had already done twice. When the problem continued, the agent closed the case without warning. The online chat disconnected three times, and the phone line charged by the minute while I listened to music.

The laptop looks good on paper, but the reviews that convinced me were clearly written after a single afternoon of use. A device at this price should not need repairs before the first holiday. I cannot recommend it, and I will not buy from this company again.
